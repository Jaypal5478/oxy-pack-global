Who we are

A paper packaging company with an export mindset from day one.

OXY PACK GLOBAL was founded in Rajkot, Gujarat in 2022 — and shipped its first export order to Africa the same year. We set out to prove that paper can do everything plastic packaging does, and do it responsibly.

Today we design, manufacture and export corrugated, printed and protective paper packaging for clients across 20+ countries. Every order is built to your specification, tested before dispatch and packed for the container — not just the shelf.

  • Engineered to spec — board grade, flute and ply matched to your load and route.
  • Export-ready — palletised, labelled and documented for customs.
  • Certified quality — ISO 9001 systems and FSC®-certified paper.

Capabilities

Structural design

Dimensions, board grade and paper inserts engineered around your product, stacking load and route.

02

Printing & branding
