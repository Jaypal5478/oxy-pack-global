FAQ

Corrugated Boxes & Cartons — common questions

Can’t find your answer? Ask our export team — we reply within one business day.

Ask a question WhatsApp
Which corrugated boxes & cartons do you manufacture?

Our range includes Corrugated export cartons (3/5/7-ply), Printed e-commerce mailer boxes, Heavy-duty boxes & octabins, Corrugated pizza boxes, Die-cut boxes, Partitions & inserts, Corrugated pallets & sheets and Shelf-ready trays. Every item is made to order to your size, material and print.

Do you make custom sizes and printed designs?

Yes. We work from your dimensions, product weight and artwork, recommend the right paper specification, and share a sample for approval before bulk production.

Which countries do you export to?

We export to buyers in 20+ countries across Africa, the Middle East, Europe, the Americas and Asia-Pacific. We palletise and load the container and handle the export paperwork.

Are your certifications suitable for international buyers?

We work to ISO 9001 quality management, use FSC®-certified paper on request, and are an IEC-registered exporter. Food-contact grades are available for food packaging.

How do I get a price?

Send your requirement through the quote form or WhatsApp. We reply within one business day with a recommended specification and price.
