Overview

About this product

Paper cups for hot and cold drinks, printed with your brand. Single-wall cups suit cold drinks, while double and ripple-wall cups keep hot drinks comfortable to hold without a separate sleeve.

Key features

  • Single, double and ripple-wall options
  • Food-grade cupstock board
  • Water-based (aqueous) barrier coating
  • Up to 6-colour flexo or offset print
  • Matching paper lids and sleeves available
