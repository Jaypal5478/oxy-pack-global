Overview

About this product

Mono-cartons for pharma, food, cosmetics and FMCG, offset printed on paperboard with sharp detail and accurate brand colours. They protect the product, carry regulatory text and sell on the shelf.

Key features

  • Offset CMYK plus Pantone printing
  • Straight tuck, reverse tuck, crash-lock and sleeve styles
  • Foil, emboss, spot UV and varnish finishes
  • Braille, barcode and batch-coding areas for pharma
  • Supplied flat and glued, ready for your packing line

What is the minimum order quantity for printed folding cartons?

The MOQ depends on size, material and printing — plain printed folding cartons usually have lower minimums than fully printed designs. Share your quantity in the quote form and we confirm the MOQ with your price.

Can you print our logo and brand on printed folding cartons?

Yes. Every order is made to your specification, including your artwork and brand colours. Printing options: Offset CMYK + Pantone.

Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We make a sample for fit, strength and print approval before bulk production starts, so you know exactly what you will receive.

Do you ship printed folding cartons to my country?

We export to buyers in 20+ countries across Africa, the Middle East, Europe, the Americas and Asia-Pacific. We palletise and load the container and handle the export paperwork.

How long does production take?

Lead time depends on quantity, materials and printing. We confirm a dispatch date with your quote and keep you updated through production and pre-shipment quality checks.
