Overview

About this product

Plates, bowls, trays and clamshells moulded from sugarcane bagasse, a by-product of sugar production. Sturdy, microwave-safe and compostable — the main replacement for foam and plastic disposables.

Key features

  • Made from sugarcane bagasse fibre
  • Microwave and freezer safe
  • Resists hot liquids and grease
  • Compostable — certificates shared on request
  • Natural white or unbleached finish
