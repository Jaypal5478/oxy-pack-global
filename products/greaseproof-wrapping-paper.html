About this product

Grease-resistant papers for wrapping burgers, sandwiches, bakery and deli food. Supplied in sheets or rolls, plain or printed with your brand, with no plastic lamination.

Key features

  • Grease-resistant without plastic film
  • Food-contact grade
  • Sheets, rolls or pre-cut wraps
  • Custom printing with food-safe inks
  • Holds up to hot food
