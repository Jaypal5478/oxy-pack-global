Overview

About this product

Self-opening square-bottom (SOS) bags stand open on the counter for fast packing. Supermarkets, bakeries and restaurants use them for groceries, bread and takeaway meals.

Key features

  • Square bottom stands upright on its own
  • With or without flat paper handles
  • Food-contact grade kraft
  • Grease-resistant option for hot food
  • Plain or printed with your logo
