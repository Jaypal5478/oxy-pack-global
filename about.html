Founded in Rajkot in 2022. Exporting from day one.

We are a paper packaging manufacturer and exporter on a mission to replace plastic with strong, well-engineered paper — for businesses in every country.

Our story

We started where most suppliers stop — at the border.

OXY PACK GLOBAL began in 2022 after seeing the same problem again and again: exporters were losing product, money and customer trust to packaging that was never designed for long-distance transit — and much of it was plastic their customers no longer wanted.

So we built a company around paper and the full journey. We pair packaging engineering with export know-how: selecting the right board, testing it against real shipping conditions and handling the paperwork that gets it through customs. Our first export order left for Africa within months of opening.

Today we serve manufacturers, brands and distributors in 20+ countries, with 500+ tonnes of monthly capacity and one promise: your goods arrive the way they left.
